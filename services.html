Heroic Services

From eight-legged intruders to evil masterminds, Mind the Gap Man handles London's smallest emergencies with the biggest possible drama. Pick your peril, pick your tier, and please stand clear of the closing cape.

The Rescues

Spider Removal

A spider the size of a Zone 1 rent bill has claimed your bath. Mind the Gap Man arrives, negotiates calmly, then carries it outside in a commemorative Oyster card holder. No spiders are harmed. Your dignity, however, is not guaranteed.

Bronze Tier

Response time: by the next Tube (usually 2–3 minutes, longer on the Northern line).

Cat in a Tree

Your cat has climbed a plane tree and is now pretending it has never met you. Our hero scales the trunk using only upper body strength and Tube strap experience, then returns the cat, who will immediately climb back up.

Silver Tier

Response time: under 30 minutes.

Wi-Fi Fixing

Buffering wheel of doom? Mind the Gap Man bravely turns the router off and on again, stares sternly at the blinking lights and, if needed, stands heroically closer to the window holding your laptop aloft.

Silver Tier

Response time: under 30 minutes.

Arch-Nemesis Defeat

A cackling villain is threatening the city, or at least your street. Our hero deploys laser eyes, a dramatic "BANG!" and a short speech about passenger etiquette. Cape billowing is included at no extra charge.

Gold Tier

Response time: immediately, faster than a Victoria line train.

Jar Opening

The pickle jar has defeated you, your flatmate and a tea towel. Mind the Gap Man applies one superhuman twist, a satisfying "POP!" and a modest nod. He will not judge you for running the lid under the hot tap first.

Bronze Tier

Response time: by the next Tube (usually 2–3 minutes, longer on the Northern line).

Queue Jumping

Stuck in the queue for brunch, the post office or the Oxford Circus escalators? Our hero politely, yet heroically, gets you to the front. Results not valid at Wimbledon, where queueing is a sacred art.

Silver Tier

Response time: under 30 minutes.
